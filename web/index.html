Antigravity Telemetry & Quota Pacing Engine • Multi-User Family Hub running on port 8778

Connect Family Members & Devices

Collect Antigravity quota from other laptops and workstations

Other family members running Google Antigravity can report their quota automatically to this hub over your local Wi-Fi or LAN.

Option 1: Windows PowerShell (Zero Install)

Run this in PowerShell on the family member's PC:

powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .\client_agent\report_usage.ps1 -ServerUrl "http://YOUR_IP:8778" -UserId "alex" -DisplayName "Alex" -Loop -IntervalMinutes 15
Option 2: Python (Windows, macOS, Linux)

Cross-platform script located in client_agent/report_usage.py:

python client_agent/report_usage.py --server "http://YOUR_IP:8778" --user alex --name "Alex" --loop --interval 15

💡 Tip: Find your machine's IP on Windows by running ipconfig (e.g. 192.168.1.X). Once submitted, the new member appears instantly in the dropdown and family cards.
